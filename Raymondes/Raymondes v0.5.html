How it works

Everything stays on your deviceYour song is decoded and processed in this tab. Nothing is uploaded.
Compare left and rightEach moment of the song is split into frequencies, and each frequency is checked for how alike it is in the left and right channels.
Split by positionSound placed in the center, like most lead vocals, goes to the vocal track. The rest stays in the instrumental. The two always add up to the original.
This is signal processing, not a neural network. It works well on typical stereo mixes, and is weaker on mono files, wide or reverb-heavy vocals, and centered snare or guitars.
Downloads are lossless 16-bit WAV. Some environments deliver them inside a ZIP.
